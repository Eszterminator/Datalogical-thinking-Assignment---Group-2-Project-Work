Rationale for Division of Labour

To develop our Goodreads Reading Planner systematically, we structured our workflow around a modular, three-tier pipeline: data curation, algorithmic logic & code, and web implementation & interface design.

Rather than overlapping on every single task, this division allowed each member to take ownership of an essential milestone while maintaining close collaboration through regular reviews. Data curation and algorithm development were approached iteratively, with data constraints informing algorithm design, and the algorithm's requirements, in turn, influencing how the data was prepared. Both these stages informed how the final project documentation was presented on the website.
